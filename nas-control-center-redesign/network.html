CONNECTIVITY / ROUTES

Network access

PRIVATE CONNECTIONS

Two ways to reach your NAS: directly over your home network, or privately through your Tailscale network.

⌂AT HOME

Local network

Use this when your computer or phone is connected to the same router or network as the NAS.

192.168.100.7
  • SSH: ssh vuky@192.168.100.7
  • Web services use this IP with their respective ports.
⌁AWAY FROM HOME

Tailscale

Install and sign in to Tailscale on the device you're using, then open the service links.

100.76.132.23
  • SSH: ssh vuky@100.76.132.23
  • Transmission is currently bound to this address only.
SECURITY

Private access basics

  • Connect to Tailscale on your client device before using remote links.
  • The Tailscale IP is a private tailnet address, not a public internet address.
  • Do not forward Cockpit, Portainer, SSH or SMB ports from your router to the public internet without a deliberate, secured setup.
  • Local IP addresses can change if your router assigns a new DHCP lease. A DHCP reservation can make the address more consistent.
