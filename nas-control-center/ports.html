Not published on the host

These ports appeared in container metadata but were not shown as host listeners in your output.

  • MySQL: 3306/tcp (container network)
  • Transmission peer: 51413/tcp and 51413/udp (container metadata)
  • Portainer agent/API: 8000/tcp, 9000/tcp (not published)
SECURITY

Don't expose everything

Avoid forwarding administrative or file-sharing ports directly from your router. Tailscale is the preferred remote path for private management access.
