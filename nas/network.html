How the NAS is connected

Home router192.168.100.1
NAS · Wi-Fi192.168.100.13
Tailscale100.76.132.23
  • Wi-Fi interface wlxb4b024d5369f is the active network route.
  • Ethernet interface enp0s25 is intentionally disabled to avoid competing routes.
  • The router has a DHCP reservation for the Wi-Fi adapter, keeping the local address stable.
  • Your router WAN address was in the CGNAT range, so Tailscale is the appropriate private remote-access path. Do not expose SSH, SMB, Cockpit or Portainer with router port forwarding.
